# Sloppi v2.0 Anti-Slop & Anti-Monoculture Evaluation Model

"Design Slop" is the unthinking repetition of visual clichés generated when a coding agent operates on autopilot.

In **Sloppi v2.0**, we guard against **two distinct generations of slop**:
1. **Generation-1 AI Slop**: The default statistical average of LLM training data (centered H1 + 2 pill buttons + floating 3D dashboard, purple/cyan blur blobs, 3-card icon grids, fake stats, `Inter` on `#0f172a`).
2. **Generation-2 "Anti-Slop" Clichés (Overfit Monoculture)**: The predictable, sterile wireframe that happens when an agent strips away all effects and repeats the same "safe" recipe on every project (flat `1px solid` bordered boxes everywhere, `11px uppercase tracking-widest` monospace labels on every section, Midnight Tungsten `#0b0d13` + Phosphor Green `#00d084` CLI terminals for every dev tool, Warm Alabaster `#fbf9f5` + Navy `#102A56` ledgers for every fintech app, and cookie-cutter 8+4 bento grids).

## Part 1: Generation-1 AI Slop Catalog (Default LLM Reflexes)

### 1. Generic Centered SaaS Hero
- **The Cliché**: Eyebrow pill badge + center-aligned H1 + 2-line subtext + two buttons ("Get Started" / "Learn More") + tilted dashboard screenshot underneath.
- **v2 Remediation**: Choose an asymmetric editorial split, full-bleed interactive instrument, typographic poster composition, or viewport-locked spatial workbench. Put the working product mechanism directly in the user's hands.

### 2. Meaningless Neon Blur Orbs & Purple/Cyan Gradients
- **The Cliché**: Splattering `filter: blur(120px)` purple/indigo/cyan radial circles into the background to fake "modern AI styling."
- **v2 Remediation**: If using atmospheric lighting, tie it to an interactive canvas (e.g., cursor-reactive shader, directional specular light source, or domain-authentic chromatic field) or use curated tactile surfaces (archival paper, brushed metal, stark monochrome, or bold solid color blocking).

### 3. Identical 3-Card Icon Grids
- **The Cliché**: Three equal rectangular cards in a row, each with a colored circle icon, a 3-word title, and a 2-sentence blurb.
- **v2 Remediation**: Represent capabilities through their actual mechanics—an interactive scrubbable timeline, an annotated architectural schematic, a live parameter sandbox, or an editorial sticky-scroll showcase.

### 4. Badge & Pill Inflation
- **The Cliché**: Putting a `rounded-full bg-primary/10 px-3 py-1` pill badge above every single heading and inside every card.
- **v2 Remediation**: Reserve pills strictly for interactive filters or live state chips. Use structural numbering (`01 / 04`), margin notes, or typographic scale alone to introduce sections.

### 5. Glassmorphism Over Nothing
- **The Cliché**: Applying `backdrop-blur-md bg-white/10` to static in-flow cards resting on a flat solid background.
- **v2 Remediation**: Use `backdrop-filter` exclusively on floating layers (sticky wayfinding, command palettes, drawers, HUD overlays) where rich visual content actively scrolls or animates underneath.

### 6. Fake Social Proof, Stock Avatars & Invented Benchmarks
- **The Cliché**: "Trusted by Fortune 500 teams", "10x Faster / 99.9% Synergy", or 3 cards with fake quotes from "Sarah J., VP of Growth."
- **v2 Remediation**: Zero fabricated claims. Show verifiable system architecture, interactive calculators, deterministic sandbox data labeled clearly, or genuine technical specifications.

### 7. Buzzword Soup & Generic CTAs
- **The Cliché**: *"Supercharge your next-gen workflow seamlessly"* with *"Get Started"* buttons repeated 6 times.
- **v2 Remediation**: Write concrete, falsifiable mechanism copy and outcome-explicit CTAs (*"Run Live Latency Trace"*, *"Open Interactive Ledger"*, *"Compile Sample Schema"*).

## Part 2: Generation-2 "Anti-Slop" Overfit Clichés (Sloppi v1 Traps to Avoid)

When agents try to avoid Gen-1 AI slop by following rigid rules, they often fall into a second set of repetitive habits. **Do not treat these as your default fallback:**

### 8. The "Flat 1px Border Wireframe" Trap
- **The Cliché**: Eliminating all shadows, gradients, and visual depth until the entire website is just flat white or dark rectangles separated by `1px solid` hairline borders (`border-slate-200` / `border-zinc-800`).
- **Why It Happens**: Over-correcting against heavy drop shadows ("The Polish Trap") by removing *all* materiality and lighting.
- **v2 Remediation**: Flat `1px` grids are only one visual dialect (Swiss Ledger). Unless your chosen direction explicitly calls for a brutalist/Swiss grid, build rich materiality: subtle SVG paper/film grain, `1px` specular top inner highlights (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`), multi-layered tonal surface shifts, tactile hardware controls, or dramatic editorial whitespace with *zero* box borders.

### 9. The "Midnight Tungsten + Phosphor Green CLI" Reflex
- **The Cliché**: Giving every developer tool, backend product, or infrastructure app a `#0b0d13` dark background, `#00d084` / `#22c55e` green accents, `JetBrains Mono`, and a fake terminal window in the hero.
- **Why It Happens**: Copy-pasting the same dev-tool archetype instead of exploring directions.
- **v2 Remediation**: Developer tools can be sunlit Swiss technical monographs (vellum paper + cobalt diagrams), tactile hardware racks (brushed aluminum + rotary controls), kinetic geometric schematics, or high-contrast editorial manifestos.

### 10. The "Warm Alabaster + Navy Ink + Tabular Mono" Reflex
- **The Cliché**: Giving every fintech, analytics, or B2B app a `#fbf9f5` / `#ffffff` background, `#102A56` navy or `#111111` ink text, `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter`, and `11px` uppercase monospace labels on every box.
- **v2 Remediation**: Explore high-contrast editorial serif broadsheets, deep emerald/obsidian institutional vaults, vibrant consumer chromatic blocking, or spatial fluid canvases.

### 11. The Automatic "8+4 Asymmetric Bento Grid" Substitution
- **The Cliché**: Replacing every 3-card feature grid with the exact same `grid-cols-12` (`col-span-8` + `col-span-4`) bento box section.
- **v2 Remediation**: Bento grids are already a saturated web trope. Alternate between horizontal scroll-snap galleries, interactive split workbenches, full-bleed diagrammatic flows, sticky scrollytelling chapters, and sculptural typographic breaks.

### 12. The `11px Uppercase Font-Mono Tracking-Widest` Addiction
- **The Cliché**: Labeling every single section, card header, and metric with `text-[11px] font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-muted-foreground`.
- **v2 Remediation**: Vary your typographic hierarchy! Use oversized italicized serif numerals, bold geometric sidebars, flush-left editorial running heads, or let the display headline speak without an eyebrow label at all.
